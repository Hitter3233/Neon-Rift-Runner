NEON RIFT RUNNER

A self-contained browser arcade game.

HOW TO PLAY
- Space / Up Arrow / W / tap the play area: jump
- Down Arrow / S / the down button: slide
- Collect yellow energy shards and dodge pink obstacles.
- Your best score is saved in this browser when localStorage is available.

RUN
Open index.html in a modern browser. No dependencies or build step are required.
